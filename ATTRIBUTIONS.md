The app does **not** contain or distribute these books. Each user adds their own copy on their own device,
where it stays:
- *Lisan ul Quran* by Amir Sohail (Lisan ul Quran Foundation).
- *Dream Textbook* and *Dream Workbook* (Bayyinah). Free copies are available from Bayyinah at
